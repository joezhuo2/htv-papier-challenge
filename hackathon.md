# Hackathon: rules, judging, categories

Event: Hack the Valley 11. Page: hack-the-valley-11.devpost.com. All times Toronto local (ET).

## Dates

- Hacking window: Friday Oct 16 9:30 PM to Sunday Oct 18 9:30 AM. Only work inside it.
- Initial submission: Saturday Oct 17, 9:00 PM, with team members and prize categories. Teams without an initial submission are not accepted. Submit a minimal valid version early Saturday and keep editing.
- Final submission: Sunday Oct 18, 9:30 AM. Target 9:00 AM.
- Judging: Sunday Oct 18, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM, in person.

## Submission

- Write-up: why you built it, tech stack, what it does.
- Public GitHub link. The project is not judged without it. README with setup, no secrets in the repo or its history.
- Demo video is optional. Devpost usually takes a YouTube or Vimeo link, check the form.
- Eligible: students, teams of 1 to 4. Joe is solo.
- Credit anything adapted (Triton tutorial kernel, Karpathy autoresearch, the workshop notebook). Plagiarism or rule-breaking means disqualification.

## Presenting

- 4 minute pitch and demo, then 1 minute of judge Q&A (technical and non-technical). Prepare a 60 second version too, since in person judging may involve repeated visits.
- Judges are encouraged to see a working demo. A broken or unfinished demo can still score well.

## General judging criteria (no point values listed)

Technicality and Quality, Design, Impact, Presentation, Inspiration, Authenticity. Impact is the weakest fit, so prepare a one sentence story: any team using AI to tune performance can hit "fast but wrong" results. Authenticity favors real logged runs and honest "recorded run" labeling.

## Categories to enter

- **Autoresearch Challenge (Papiers):** core entry. Prize: one month of Claude Max 5x. Papiers' criteria: autonomous research, human-agent collaboration, technical execution, originality, evidence and demonstration. The project must show a completed experiment with an evaluation and an agent chosen next step, how human input shapes the work, and the evidence gathered.
- **Best Solo Hack:** enter. Competes only against solo teams.
- **Top 3:** automatic for every team.
- **DEV Writing Challenge ($100):** optional, only after the writeup is done. Requires publishing the project on DEV.
- **Best Beginner Hack:** only if Joe meets the rules' definition, which the page does not state.

Skip: Best AI for Social Impact, Healthcare, Climate, FinTech, Catch It Early, FDM, School of Cities, Project KAZE, Gemini, Solana, Tiger Data, Vultr, GoDaddy. No NVIDIA prize exists, but a listed judge is an NVIDIA engineer, so mention Triton and NIM.
